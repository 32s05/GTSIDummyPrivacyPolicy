Data Deletion Instructions

Genesis Transport respects your privacy and your right to request the deletion of your personal data.

How to Request Data Deletion

If you want to request the deletion of personal data associated with our application, please email us at:

gtsi.genesistrans@gmail.com

Use the subject line "Data Deletion Request" and include enough information for us to identify your request. Please do not send your password.

What Happens Next?

We will review your request, verify it when necessary, and delete the personal data we control that we are not required to retain for legal or legitimate business purposes. We will notify you when the request has been processed.

Facebook Data

If you want to remove the app's access to your Facebook account, you can also remove the application from your Facebook account's Business Integrations settings. Removing access may not automatically delete data already stored by our application; contact us to request deletion of that data.

Contact

For questions about data deletion, contact us at gtsi.genesistrans@gmail.com.
